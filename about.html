About me

Get to know me

I'm a CS student who spent a year and a half studying mechanical engineering before changing course to follow what I was actually built for.

I got into software because it felt like magic. The idea that you can think of something, and make it real. That feeling hasn't gone away.

I'm a freelance software developer with over 14 months of experience. I build custom software and AI integrations, the kind of tools that don't exist yet.

Typersian started with my own frustration. I grew up more comfortable typing in Latin script than Persian, which made writing in Persian slower and more painful than it needed to be. No existing tool solved it the way I needed: system-wide, across every app, in real time. So I built it myself. It became the world's first system-wide Finglish to Persian converter: Typersian.

Most recently I built an AI-powered WhatsApp and Telegram bot for an Irish client — automating bookings, enquiries, and operations through natural language. A real client, a real problem, a real solution.

If you have a problem that existing software can't solve, let's talk.
